Lecture 6 - Mastering the BACCM framework

The BACCM = Business Analysis Core Concept Model, is the backbone of the BABOK guide and is the foundational element of business analysis.

Its a way of thinking rather than a framework.

The backbone provides a structured lens through which all BA work can be viewed, ensuring that every decision, action, and deliverable aligns with organizational goals and stakeholders needs. 


6 core concept of BACCM:

1. Change
2. Need
3. Solution
4. Stakeholder
5. Value
6. Context


Change
Heart of business analysis work. As BA, we help to move organization from current state to desired future state.

BACCM reminds us that no matter how large or small the initiative, our role is to define, support and enable change in a way that delivers meaningful results.

Change is always driven by a need. 


Need

A need represents the problem or opportunity that sparks the desire for change. 

As BA, our work starts with identifying the need and ensuring it is clearly understood and agreed upon by stakeholders. 

Defining a need is crucial as it answers the why behind the change and it keeps the change focused on solving the right problem to address a need.

To address a need, we create a solution.

Solution
A solution is how we solve a problem or we seize the opportunity presented by that need. Can have many form such as new software or updated process or structural change within and organization.


Value

It is the positive impact a solution creates for the organization and for its stakeholders. It can be financial gain, improved customer satisfaction, operational efficiency or even regulatory compliance. 


Stakeholders

These are the individuals or groups affected by or involved in the change. Managing stakeholders effectively is one of the most critical aspects of business analysis.  

Context
Which refers to the environment where the change occurs. It includes factors like organizational culture, managing conditions. and regulatory requirements. 
Understanding context is essential because it influences how needs, solutions and values are defined and delivered. 



Relationship between BACCM concept

A need is addressed by change
A change is made with the solution
Value is the success gained from the solution
Stakeholders are those taking part in or affected by that change
Context serves as the foundation influencing every aspect of how that solution is designed, developed and implemented. 


These core concepts serve as a constant reminder that business analysis is not just about solving problems, it's about creating meaningful and sustainable value.
