Lecture 1 - Introducing the BABOK Essential Course

BABOK - Business Analysis Body Of Knowledge

BABOK Serves as the globally recognized standard for business analysis professionals. 


This course will teach us foundational concept.


The BABOK guide isn't just a collection of definitions, its a resource that helps is equip BA with a structured framework to understand and excel in their role. 

Think of it as a professional compass offering guidance on what a BA does and how it does it effectively.

We will gain knowledge of 6 knowledge areas that span the life cycle of business analysis from the following:

1. Business analysis planning and monitoring.
2. Elicitation and collaboration.
3. Requirements Life Cycle Management.
4. Strategy Analysis.
5. Requirements Analysis and Design Definitions.
6. Solution Evaluation. 


We will also aim to build a strong foundation in the Business Analysis Core Concept Model (BACCM).
